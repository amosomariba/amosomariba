## 🌱 About Me

I'm a passionate **backend developer** currently learning through the **ALX Software Engineering Program**. I started my journey into software engineering to **understand how problem-solving works** and to **develop solutions that help my community**.

What drives me is the opportunity to use technology to address real-life challenges—particularly in the health space. I'm especially inspired to help people struggling with **diet and obesity-related issues**. My goal is to build **AI-powered tools** that:
